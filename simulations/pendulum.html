Period of a Pendulum

T = 2π√(L/g)

For small angles (< 15°), the period depends only on length and gravity — not on mass or amplitude. This is isochronism.

Energy

E = ½mv² + mgh

Energy continuously converts between kinetic and potential. Total mechanical energy is conserved (assuming no damping).

Large-Angle Correction

T ≈ 2π√(L/g) · (1 + θ²/16)

For angles above ~15°, the small-angle approximation breaks down. The true period increases slightly with amplitude.
