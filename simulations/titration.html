How Titration Works

A solution of known concentration (the titrant) is added to a solution of unknown concentration (the analyte) until the reaction is complete. The equivalence point is reached when moles of acid = moles of base.

Equivalence Point

MaVa = MbVb

For a 1:1 reaction, moles of acid = moles of base at the equivalence point. The pH at this point depends on the acid/base strength.

pH at Equivalence

Strong-Strong: pH = 7 (neutral)
Weak Acid + Strong Base: pH > 7 (basic)
Strong Acid + Weak Base: pH < 7 (acidic)
The indicator should change colour near the equivalence point pH.

Phenolphthalein

Changes from colourless to pink at pH 8.2–10.0. Best for strong base titrations. Not suitable for weak base titrations (equivalence pH is too low).
